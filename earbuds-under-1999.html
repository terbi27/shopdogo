How to Choose Wireless Earbuds Under ₹2,000

1. Battery life

Look at both the earbuds' playback time and the total battery life provided by the charging case.

2. Sound quality

Consider the type of sound you prefer. Bass-heavy tuning may suit some listeners, while others may prefer a more balanced sound.

3. Microphone quality

If you frequently make calls, microphone performance should be an important part of your decision.

4. Comfort and fit

Earbuds can sound good but still be uncomfortable if they do not fit your ears properly. Fit is especially important for long listening sessions.

5. ANC and transparency modes

If you travel frequently or use earbuds in noisy environments, active noise cancellation can be useful.
